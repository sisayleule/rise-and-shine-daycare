Our Programs

Age-Appropriate Care for Every Stage

Each program is designed to nurture your child's developmental milestones in a safe, stimulating environment.

Infants

6 – 12 months

Feeding:
Provide appropriate complementary food and breastfeeding support.

Hygiene:
Keep the baby, clothes, feeding materials, and sleeping area clean.

Sleep:
Provide safe and comfortable rest and sleep.

Development:
Talk, sing, smile, hold, and play with the baby.

Movement:
Provide safe space for crawling, sitting, standing, and reaching.

Safety:
Closely supervise the baby and keep small or dangerous objects away.

Young Toddlers

1 – 2 years

Feeding:
Provide nutritious meals and snacks and encourage self-feeding.

Hygiene:
Support handwashing, diapering/toileting, bathing, and clean clothing.

Language:
Talk, name objects, sing songs, and encourage simple words.

Movement:
Encourage walking, running, climbing, and simple physical play.

Play:
Provide simple toys, picture books, music, and safe exploration.

Independence:
Encourage children to do simple things by themselves.

Toddlers

2 – 3 years

Feeding:
Provide balanced local foods and encourage children to eat independently.

Hygiene:
Teach handwashing, toilet use, and personal cleanliness.

Language:
Encourage speaking, listening, naming, and simple conversations.

Learning through play:
Use local materials, toys, songs, stories, drawing, and simple games.

Social skills:
Teach sharing, taking turns, greeting, and playing with others.

Independence:
Encourage dressing, eating, cleaning up, and other simple self-help activities.

Preschool Age

3 – 4 years

Learning:
Introduce simple numbers, letters, colors, shapes, and everyday concepts.

Language:
Develop speaking, listening, storytelling, and vocabulary.

Social development:
Teach cooperation, sharing, respect, and following simple rules.

Creativity:
Provide drawing, coloring, music, singing, storytelling, and role play.

Physical development:
Provide outdoor play, running, jumping, balancing, and other physical activities.

School readiness:
Develop attention, participation, self-confidence, independence, and simple learning routines.

Health & Safety

Your Child's Well-being Comes First

We maintain the highest standards of safety, cleanliness, and nutrition across every aspect of care.

Secure Entry System

Keycard access with video intercom for authorized pick-up only

Live Camera Monitoring

Cameras in all classrooms and common areas for real-time oversight

Childproofed Spaces

Soft flooring, covered outlets, secured furniture, and gated areas

Fire & Safety Drills

Regular emergency drills and up-to-date fire safety equipment

Daily Health Checks

Temperature checks and visual health screening at drop-off every morning

Illness Exclusion Policy

Children with fever, vomiting, or contagious symptoms stay home until symptom-free for 24 hours

Parent Communication

Immediate notification to parents if a child shows signs of illness during the day

Staff Health Standards

Teachers follow the same illness guidelines and model good hygiene habits

Frequent Handwashing

Before/after meals, after diaper changes, after outdoor play, and regularly throughout the day

Daily Sanitization

Toys, surfaces, and equipment cleaned and sanitized multiple times per day

Diapering Protocol

Strict changing procedures with gloves, sanitization, and logged changes

Ventilation & Air Quality

HVAC filtered air and regular fresh air circulation throughout the facility

Balanced, Nutritious Meals

Breakfast, lunch, and afternoon snack prepared fresh daily with whole foods

Allergy Management

Individual allergy plans posted in kitchen and classrooms, with alternatives always available

Self-Feeding Support

Age-appropriate utensils and gentle encouragement for developing independence

Parent-Supplied Options

Parents may provide breastmilk, formula, or special dietary items; we follow individual feeding plans

Safe Sleep Practices

Back-to-sleep positioning, firm mattresses, no loose bedding — following AAP guidelines

Continuous Monitoring

Teachers actively supervise nap time with regular visual checks every few minutes

Individualized Schedules

Each child follows their own nap schedule based on age and parent preferences

Calming Environment

Dimmed lights, soft music, and cozy individual sleep spaces for restful naps
